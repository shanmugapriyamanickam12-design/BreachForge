## **Understanding the SOC**

## SCENARIO 1 :

A company has the following systems:

- Employee laptops
- Email accounts
- Company website
- Servers

### Answer the following:

**1. Name ONE type of activity the SOC could monitor for each system.**
   
      - Employee laptops - Unusual login attempts.
      - Email accounts - Phishing mails, unusual login attempts from different locations.
      - Company websites - unauthorized admin login attempts, network traffic.
      - Servers - Suspicious network activity.

**2. Which one of these systems would you check if you received an alert
   about a suspicious employee login?**
   
      Email account - I want to verify the employee's identity first.

**3.In your own words, what does an L1 SOC Analyst do?**

      A L1 SOC Analyst will monitor security alerts and initiate the first step of investigation of the incident, 
      checks the basic information about the incident and escalates the issue to a senior authority if necessary.

## SCENARIO 2

A user account has 20 failed login attempts within two minutes.

### Answer the following:

**1. Why might this activity need attention?**
   
      This needs attention because 20 failed login attempts in two minutes is unusual. And maybe someone(attacker)
      is trying to gain unauthorized access.

**2. What would you check before deciding whether it is a real security
   problem?**
   
      First I would check who is trying to login, whether it's the company employee or not.
      After that where is this happening?(location)
      Was there a successful login afterwards? these helps to understand 
      whether it is a genuine user(forgotten password) or a possible attack.

**3.Would you immediately say that this is definitely an attack?
   Explain briefly.**
   
      No. I would check the basic information of the incident after that only 
      we can confirm whether it is an attack or not 
      since there are possibilities that it can be a mistake of user.
